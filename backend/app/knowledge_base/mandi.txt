Mandi prices refer to the selling prices of crops in agricultural markets.

Prices can change every day depending on market conditions.

Sample mandi prices:

Rice: ₹2,200 per quintal
Wheat: ₹2,450 per quintal
Cotton: ₹7,100 per quintal
Maize: ₹2,100 per quintal
Tomato: ₹1,800 per quintal
Potato: ₹1,600 per quintal
Onion: ₹2,000 per quintal

Farmers should check the latest mandi prices before selling their crops.

These prices are dummy/sample data for demonstration purposes and should not be treated as live market prices.
